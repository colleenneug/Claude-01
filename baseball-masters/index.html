How to play

Pitching: pick a pitch, move the mouse (or arrow keys) to aim inside the zone, click / Space to start the accuracy meter, click / Space again to lock it in — stop it near the top for a clean pitch.

Hitting: move the reticle (mouse or arrow keys) onto the incoming ball and swing at the right instant — Space normal, Z contact, X power, C bunt.
